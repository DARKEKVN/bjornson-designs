Kitchen cabinet FAQ

Planning kitchen cabinets in Calgary.

What kitchen cabinet details can Bjornson help plan?

Layouts, islands, pantry storage, appliance integration, door styles, finishes, hardware, drawer access, and room-specific storage are all part of the conversation.

Can you help with a kitchen renovation?

Yes. The team supports renovation cabinetry and helps keep design decisions connected to budget, schedule, delivery, and installation.

Can I compare finishes before deciding?

Yes. The showroom helps clients compare painted cabinets, wood tones, door profiles, hardware, and material palettes.

Where should I start?
