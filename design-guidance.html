Cabinet design FAQ

What design guidance covers.

Can Bjornson help choose cabinet finishes?

Yes. The showroom process helps compare cabinet door profiles, wood tones, painted colors, hardware, counters, and material combinations.

Does design guidance include storage planning?

Yes. Cabinet layout, drawers, pantries, islands, vanities, mudrooms, laundry cabinetry, and room-specific storage are part of the planning conversation.

Can design guidance help avoid renovation mistakes?

Yes. Reviewing layout, storage, measurements, finishes, and install realities early can prevent awkward proportions, missed clearances, and rushed material choices.

Is design guidance useful for new builds?

Yes. New builds benefit from organized cabinet selections, builder timeline awareness, appliance planning, and finish coordination across the home.
