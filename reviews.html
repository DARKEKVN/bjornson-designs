Review FAQ

What to look for in cabinet company reviews.

What review signals matter for cabinet projects?

Look for comments about design guidance, communication, installation quality, service, follow-up, and whether the company listened to the homeowner.

Do reviews help compare cabinet makers?

Yes. Cabinetry is a detailed process, so reviews can reveal how a team handles ideas, timelines, changes, site realities, and service after install.

Are the reviews connected to real services?

The review themes align with Bjornson's service areas: kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities, design guidance, delivery, installation, service, and warranty.

What should I ask when I call?

Ask how the design process works, what decisions happen in the showroom, and how delivery, installation, service, and warranty are coordinated.
