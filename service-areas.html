Service area FAQ

Questions about local cabinet service.

Does Bjornson Designs serve Calgary?

Yes. Bjornson Designs serves Calgary homeowners with kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities, built-ins, design guidance, delivery and installation support.

Where is the showroom located?

Bjornson Designs is located at 124 - 292188 Wagon Wheel Blvd in Rocky View County, AB T4A 3P8.

Do you serve communities outside Calgary?

Yes. The service area includes Rocky View County and surrounding communities such as Airdrie, Cochrane, Chestermere and Okotoks.

Can I request a callback if I am not sure I am in the service area?

Yes. Use the callback form and include your community, project type and timeline so the team can follow up.
