Portfolio FAQ

How to use the gallery while planning cabinets.

Can I browse only kitchen cabinets?

Yes. Use the kitchen cabinet gallery to focus on islands, perimeter cabinets, pantries, appliance integration, cabinet colors, and kitchen storage ideas.

Does the portfolio include bathroom cabinetry?

Yes. The bathroom vanity page groups ensuite vanities, powder room cabinetry, storage details, and room-scale cabinet ideas.

Can showroom photos help with finishes?

Yes. Showroom detail images are useful for comparing door profiles, hardware, wood tones, painted finishes, and material combinations before a design meeting.

Can I reference gallery photos in a callback request?

Yes. Mention the room type, cabinet style, finish direction, and any gallery examples that match the feel of your Calgary-area project.
