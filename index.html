Calgary cabinetry FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before choosing custom cabinets.

Do you design custom kitchen cabinets in Calgary?

Yes. Bjornson Designs serves Calgary and surrounding areas with kitchen cabinet design, material selection, production coordination, delivery, installation, service, and warranty support.

Can you help with renovations and new builds?

Yes. The team supports both renovation projects and dream-home builds, helping keep cabinetry decisions organized, on track, and aligned with budget.

Do you have a cabinet showroom?

Yes. The showroom helps clients compare cabinet door styles, finishes, wood tones, painted cabinetry, hardware, storage ideas, and material palettes before production.

What cabinet services do you offer?

Core services include custom kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities, cabinet design guidance, showroom consultations, delivery, installation, service, and warranty coordination.

Where is Bjornson Designs located?

Bjornson Designs is located at 124 - 292188 Wagon Wheel Blvd in Rocky View County, serving Calgary and nearby Alberta communities.

How should I start a cabinetry project?

Start with a callback request and include the room, renovation or new-build status, rough timeline, must-have storage, preferred finishes, and any inspiration photos.
