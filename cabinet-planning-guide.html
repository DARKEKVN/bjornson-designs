Cabinet planning guide

How to plan custom cabinets before choosing finishes.

A practical guide for Calgary homeowners comparing cabinet makers, kitchen cabinet options, bathroom vanities, built-ins, and installation support.

Before the showroom

Better cabinet decisions start with the way the room needs to work.

Cabinetry planning is easier when function leads the conversation. Before comparing door styles or paint colors, clarify how the room is used, what is frustrating now, what needs to be stored, what appliances or plumbing affect the layout, and what timeline the project needs to respect.

This guide gives searchers useful planning advice while pointing them toward Bjornson's portfolio, showroom details, design guidance, and callback form.

Cabinet planning checklist

What to prepare before a cabinet design consultation.

01

Define the room and project type

Kitchen renovation, bathroom vanity, new build, pantry, mudroom, laundry room, built-in feature, or whole-home cabinetry package.

02

List storage problems

Note what is hard to reach, what does not fit, where clutter gathers, and which drawers, shelves or tall cabinets would improve daily use.

03

Collect appliance and fixture details

Appliances, sinks, plumbing locations, counters, flooring and site conditions all affect cabinet layout and installation planning.

04

Compare finishes in context

Use the showroom to compare painted cabinetry, wood tones, door profiles, hardware, counters and material pairings under real lighting.

05

Ask about delivery and install

A strong cabinet company should explain site readiness, production timing, delivery, installation coordination, service and warranty support.

06

Review the portfolio

Compare kitchens, bathroom vanities, showroom details and storage solutions that are close to the room you are planning.

Planning FAQ

Choosing custom cabinets with fewer surprises.

What should I decide before a cabinet consultation?

Think about the room, storage problems, appliance plans, renovation or new-build timeline, finish preferences, budget range and examples of cabinetry you like.

How do I choose a cabinet maker in Calgary?

Look for design guidance, showroom support, clear communication, installation coordination, service follow-up, warranty support and a portfolio of relevant work.

Should I choose finishes before layout?

Layout and function should guide finish decisions. Once storage, flow and proportions are clear, finishes can be selected with more confidence.

Why does installation matter when planning cabinets?

Installation affects clearances, appliance fit, alignment, doors, drawers, counters and the daily usability of the finished cabinetry.
