Cabinet makers and design support

A custom cabinet project is only as strong as the decisions behind it.

Bjornson Designs helps Calgary-area homeowners turn inspiration into clear cabinet plans. The team considers how each room is used, what needs to be stored, how finishes work together, and how cabinetry will move from drawings into production and installation.

That full-service approach matters whether the project is a kitchen renovation, a bathroom vanity, a mudroom storage wall, a laundry room, a built-in feature, or a new-home cabinetry package.

Why custom cabinetry

Custom cabinets solve more than surface style.

Custom cabinet FAQ

Common questions before starting custom cabinets.

What custom cabinet services does Bjornson offer?

Bjornson Designs plans custom kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities, built-ins, storage cabinetry, showroom selections, delivery, installation, service and warranty support.

Do you work on Calgary renovations and new builds?

Yes. The team supports renovation projects and new-home cabinetry packages across Calgary, Rocky View County and surrounding communities.

Can I compare cabinet finishes before deciding?

Yes. The showroom helps clients compare door profiles, painted finishes, wood tones, hardware, storage ideas and material palettes.

What makes a custom cabinet project successful?

A strong project connects layout, storage, materials, budget, production timing, delivery, installation and follow-up service before cabinetry is ordered.
