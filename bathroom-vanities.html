Bathroom vanities Calgary

Bathroom cabinets and vanities that make compact spaces work harder.

Custom bathroom vanities need the right balance of storage, moisture-aware finishes, counter space, drawer access, plumbing clearances, hardware, and visual proportion. Bjornson Designs helps homeowners plan ensuite vanities, powder room cabinetry, linen storage, floating vanity looks, and bathroom cabinet details.

The dedicated bathroom gallery makes it easier to compare vanity styles, cabinet colors, door profiles, hardware, and room-scale ideas before booking a design conversation. Good bathroom cabinetry should feel refined, but it also needs to solve the everyday storage pressure of a smaller room.

Bathroom vanity FAQ

Choosing bathroom cabinetry that fits the room.

Do custom bathroom vanities improve storage?

Yes. Drawer layout, linen storage, cabinet depth, plumbing clearances, and counter planning can make compact bathrooms work harder.

Can Bjornson help with ensuite vanities?

Yes. Ensuite vanities, powder room cabinetry, linen cabinets, and bathroom storage details can all be discussed during design guidance.

Can I match bathroom cabinetry to kitchen cabinets?

Yes. The showroom process can help coordinate cabinet finishes, door profiles, and hardware across kitchens, bathrooms, and other rooms.

How do I start a bathroom vanity project?

Use the callback form and include whether it is an ensuite, powder room, renovation, new build, or whole-home cabinetry package.
