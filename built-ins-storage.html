Built-in cabinet FAQ

Planning storage cabinetry around daily use.

What rooms can use built-in cabinets?

Built-ins can support mudrooms, laundry rooms, pantries, living rooms, offices, entries, hallways, storage walls and whole-home cabinetry packages.

Can built-ins match kitchen cabinetry?

Yes. Door profiles, cabinet finishes, hardware and materials can be coordinated with kitchen cabinets, vanities and other rooms.

Are storage cabinets useful in renovations?

Yes. Renovations are a strong opportunity to solve daily storage problems with cabinetry designed for the room and the way the household uses it.

How do I start a built-in cabinet project?

Share the room, measurements if available, storage problems, preferred finishes and timeline through the callback form.
